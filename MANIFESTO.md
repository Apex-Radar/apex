# Apex — Manifesto

Apex is the AEO operating system for Claude Code.

Two questions decide your AI visibility:

1. Are you understood?  (AI Recognition)
2. Are you cited?       (AI Retrieval)

Apex grades both, fixes the work you control, and proves the score moved.

**AAIV first. Citations follow.**

The work you control is finite, knowable, and shippable. Citations compound
on top of that work over months, not minutes. We grade the work first because
it's the only score that's honest on day one. Citations come graded after
your domain has had time to be seen.

You bring your own keys. Apex never proxies LLM calls and never pays your
provider bill. The only cost surface we run on your behalf is Radar's audit
infrastructure, and that's metered against your account.

We stand on the shoulders of `aeo.js`, `goose-aeo`, and the `aeo-strategist`
skill. They are credited in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`. Contributions to this open
repo stay open, forever.
